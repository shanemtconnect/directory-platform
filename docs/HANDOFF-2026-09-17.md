- Neighbourhood slugs unique **per town**, not site-wide (every city has a "city-centre").

- Consent wording when `leadMarketplace` is on: *"We pass your request to matching businesses, who may
  pay us to receive it. We never share it with anyone else."* (`lib/leads/consent.ts`).

  are purged after 90 days (`leads.retainSoldDays`).
